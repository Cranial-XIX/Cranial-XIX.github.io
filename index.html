Bo Liu

Be a scientist out of interest :)

Member of Technical Staff · Recursive Superintelligence

I work on pretraining and continual learning at Recursive Super­intelligence. My research interest lies in designing theoretically sound algorithms and neural architectures for agents with persistent memory and continual improvement. Before Recursive, I worked on post-training at Microsoft Superintelligence and Meta Superintelligence Lab.

I received my Ph.D. from UT Austin, where I was fortunate to be co-advised by Prof. Peter Stone and Prof. Qiang Liu. Before that, I completed my master's degree at Stanford and my undergraduate degree at Johns Hopkins.

Highlights

  1. Flint

    Recursive's local coding model, outperforming Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on coding and SWE tasks. Core contributor across pretraining, mid-training and post-training.

  2. MAI-Code-1-Flash

    Core contributor to post-training: RL and distillation recipes.

  3. Llama 4 Reasoning

    Core contributor to post-training: large-scale RL runs and recipes, and the initial RL infrastructure for TBD Lab.

  4. Architectures

    Recurrent architectures for persistent memory. Longhorn casts state-space models as amortized online learners; Maglev makes recurrent transformers with memory parallelizable to train.

  5. Optimization

    Async Local-SGD at DeepMind, Distributed Lion, a Lyapunov view of Lion, and Cautious Optimizers.

  6. LIBERO

    Lifelong robot-learning benchmark; a widely used evaluation suite for VLA models, with 2k+ GitHub stars.

  7. LLM+P

    The first framework to pair LLMs with classical PDDL planners for correct or optimal long-horizon plans.
